## What is Merlin?

Merlin is an **AI-powered disposable email validator** designed to help developers and business owners protect their systems from "burner" emails. Unlike traditional blacklist-based validators, Merlin uses advanced heuristics and real-time AI analysis to determine the legitimacy of a domain.

## Why Disposable Email Validation Matters

Disposable email addresses (DEAs) are often used to bypass trial limits, create multiple accounts, or perform fraudulent activities. By detecting these at the point of entry, you can:

1. **Improve Lead Quality**: Focus on real users who use their primary email addresses.
2. **Reduce Spam**: Prevent automated bots from polluting your database.
3. **Save Infrastructure Costs**: Don't waste resources on temporary accounts that will never be used.
